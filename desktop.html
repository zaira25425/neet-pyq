Please read the instructions carefully

General Instructions:

1. Total duration of examination is 200 minutes (3 hours 20 minutes).

2. The clock will be set at the server. The countdown timer will display the remaining time. When the timer reaches zero, the examination will end by itself.

3. The Question Palette displayed on the right side will show the status of each question using the following symbols:

1
Not visited yet
2
Not answered
3
Answered
4
Marked for review (not answered)
5
Answered & Marked for Review (will be evaluated)
Navigating to a Question:

a. Click on the question number in the Question Palette to go directly to that question.

b. Click Save & Next to save your answer and move to the next question.

c. Click Mark for Review & Next to save, mark for review, and move to the next question.

Answering a Question:

a. Click on the option button to select your answer.

b. Click Clear Response to deselect your answer.

c. You MUST click Save & Next to save your answer.

Marking Scheme:

+4 for each correct answer, -1 for each incorrect answer, 0 for unattempted questions.

Note: Some questions were originally image-based (diagrams, structures, circuits); the text is preserved for practice. Every question has a "Google the question" link you can use if you suspect any error in the question or the answer key.
